Why It Feels Different

Made to turn complex eye screening into calm, practical guidance.

OptiGuard AI is designed for reassurance as much as speed. Instead of dumping raw results on the screen, it organizes insights into a readable dashboard, keeps your scan history together, and gives you a simple place to ask follow-up questions.

Understandable output Risk labels, trends, and supportive explanations instead of technical noise.
Continuous monitoring Track changes over time and revisit earlier scans when something feels off.
Accessible by design Free for patients and built to support screening access in underserved communities.
Early Attention Matters

Many eye conditions progress quietly. Faster screening can help people seek clinical care before symptoms become severe.
